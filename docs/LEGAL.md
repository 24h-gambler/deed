# Legal and Rights

> This is a product design document, not legal advice. Every category and clause must be reviewed by counsel in the relevant jurisdiction before launch.

---

## 1. Principles

1. **License, not assignment.** Creators keep ownership. Deed and buyers get a defined license.
2. **Consent is specific.** Purpose, scope, term, territory, third-party provision, and cross-border transfer are explicit.
3. **Purpose limitation.** Data is used for the buyer's stated AI training/evaluation purpose, not for resale or re-identification.
4. **Deletion and withdrawal.** Creators can withdraw future use; deletion is handled per contract and law.
5. **Provenance.** Every dataset carries consent records, source, and license terms.
6. **No coercion.** No "upload or be sued." No dark patterns.

---

## 2. Data categories

| Category | Policy |
|---|---|
| **Creator-owned content** (photos, voice, text, annotations) | Allowed with a license and consent |
| **Personal data** (name, contact, face, voice) | Explicit consent; purpose limitation; PII minimization |
| **Sensitive data** (health, biometric, sexual, political) | Explicit separate consent; often excluded |
| **Children's data** | Excluded, or guardian consent with strict controls |
| **Third-party content** | Creator must hold rights or have permission; otherwise excluded |
| **Public data** | Allowed only if license/terms permit AI training and redistribution |
| **Illegal content** (CSAM, non-consensual intimate data, stolen data) | Zero tolerance; report and remove |
| **AI-generated data** | Labeled; not accepted as human data without disclosure |

---

## 3. Creator warranties

By submitting, the creator warrants:

- They own the data or have written permission to license it.
- They have obtained consent from any identifiable person.
- The data does not contain illegal content or infringe third-party rights.
- They will cooperate with deletion/withdrawal requests.
- They understand the license scope and revenue share.

---

## 4. License terms (template)

| Term | Default |
|---|---|
| Grant | Non-exclusive, sublicensable to the buyer |
| Purpose | AI training, evaluation, fine-tuning |
| Term | 2 years, renewable |
| Territory | Global |
| Resale | Prohibited |
| Re-identification | Prohibited |
| Attribution | Where required by law/contract |
| Deletion | On request, subject to legal retention |
| Revenue share | 50–70% of net license revenue |

---

## 5. Buyer obligations

- Use data only for the licensed purpose.
- Do not re-identify, resell, or redistribute.
- Maintain security and access controls.
- Report misuse or breach.
- Comply with applicable AI/privacy laws.
- Accept audit rights for sensitive datasets.

---

## 6. Platform obligations

- Verify creator identity and rights representation.
- Run PII/duplicate/illegal-content checks.
- Maintain consent and provenance records.
- Pay creators accurately and on time.
- Handle deletion/withdrawal requests.
- Report illegal content.
- Maintain a takedown process.

---

## 7. Revenue share and tax

- **Net license revenue** = gross license fee − payment fees − taxes − direct QC costs.
- Creator share: 50–70%, stated in the request and the license.
- Payout: KRW/fiat, monthly or per dataset, with withholding/tax documents.
- Minimum payout threshold to control transfer fees.
- No token, no points convertible to cash, no price appreciation.

---

## 8. Cross-border

- Korea: PIPA consent, cross-border transfer disclosure, retention.
- EU: GDPR lawful basis, data subject rights, transfer mechanisms.
- US: state privacy laws, contract terms, no sale without consent.
- China: PIPL, separate consent, security assessment where required.

---

## 9. Recordkeeping

- Consent record: who, what, when, purpose, scope, withdrawal.
- Provenance: source, file hash, QC result, reviewer.
- License: buyer, scope, term, fee, payout.
- Deletion: request, action, timestamp.
- Retention: per law and contract; default 5 years for transaction records.

---

## 10. Open questions for counsel

1. Is Deed a data broker, marketplace, or licensor under Korean law?
2. What consent language is required for AI training and sublicensing?
3. How to handle sensitive data and children's data.
4. Cross-border transfer mechanisms for US/EU buyers.
5. Tax treatment of creator payouts (business income vs. other).
6. Liability allocation for creator misrepresentation.
7. Takedown/notice-and-takedown obligations.
8. Whether buyer use of data in models triggers additional rights.
