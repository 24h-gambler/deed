# YC Application Narrative

**Company** Deed  
**One-liner** The licensed data supply layer for vertical AI.  
**RFS fit** Vertical Foundation Models / Own Your Intelligence — high-quality, rights-cleared, vertical-specific data is the bottleneck.

**Two-line summary** Deed is the demand-pulled data marketplace for vertical AI: buyers post a data request, creators submit rights-cleared data, and get paid when it is licensed. No token, no user-to-user trading, no data dump.

---

## 1. What are you building?

A vertical AI lab needs 1,000 Korean skincare reviews with face photos, consent, and provenance. Today it scrapes (risky), hires a general vendor (slow, expensive), or collects in-house (does not scale).

Deed flips the process:
1. The buyer posts a **Data Request** with spec, quantity, quality bar, rights, and budget.
2. Creators submit data against the request.
3. Deed runs AI + human QC: quality, duplicates, PII, illegal content, consent.
4. Deed licenses the accepted dataset to the buyer.
5. Creators get 50–70% of net license revenue in KRW/fiat.

The buyer gets a clean dataset with provenance. The creator gets paid without giving up ownership. Deed gets 30–40%.

---

## 2. The problem

**For buyers:** vertical AI is bottlenecked by data, not models.
- General vendors sell general data; vertical quality is weak.
- Scraping is legally risky and has no consent.
- In-house collection is slow and expensive.
- Legal review blocks datasets without provenance.

**For creators:** they have the data but no clean monetization path.
- Generic marketplaces pay pennies and obscure usage.
- They do not want to assign ownership.
- They do not know which buyers want their data.

**The gap:** a demand-pulled, rights-cleared, quality-verified supply layer.

---

## 3. Why existing solutions fail

| Alternative | Why it fails |
|---|---|
| Scale / Surge / Mercor | general-purpose, expensive, not vertical |
| Toloka / Appen | global crowd, quality variance |
| Scraping vendors | no consent, no provenance, legal risk |
| In-house | slow, does not scale |
| Crypto data marketplaces | speculation, tokens, no B2B license |
| Generic consumer data marketplaces | random supply, low prices, unclear rights |

The data is not scarce. **Clean, licensed, verified supply is.**

---

## 4. Solution

**Demand-pulled data licensing.**

| Module | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Request** | buyer spec, quantity, quality, rights, budget, deadline |
| **Submission** | creator upload, consent, metadata, hash |
| **QC** | duplicate, PII, illegal content, quality, human review |
| **License** | dataset assembly, license terms, provenance bundle |
| **Payout** | 50–70% revenue share, KRW/fiat, tax withholding |
| **Ledger** | double-entry, client funds, creator payable, platform revenue |

No token. No user-to-user trading. No coercion.

---

## 5. Why now

- **Vertical models need vertical data.** General models are commoditizing; proprietary data is the differentiator.
- **AI data is running out.** Labs are paying for licensed, human-generated, domain-specific data.
- **Privacy regulation is tightening.** PIPA/GDPR make consent and provenance mandatory.
- **Creators need revenue.** The creator economy is looking beyond ads/subscriptions.
- **Korea/Asia data is scarce.** Korean voice, K-beauty, and expert annotations are hard to source legally.

---

## 6. Why us

- **Domain:** we understand data rights, consent, and QC — not just marketplace UX.
- **Supply-first:** we start with 30–50 creators in one domain, not a generic upload button.
- **Demand-first:** we start with 3 buyers who have a live data need and a budget.
- **Legal-first:** license, consent, provenance, deletion. This is why buyers trust us.

---
