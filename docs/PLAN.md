# Execution Plan

**Goal** Prove demand-pulled data licensing in 90 days: 3 buyers with paid requests, 100 creators, 1,000 accepted data units, first revenue, zero rights incidents.

---

## 1. 90-day plan

| Week | Focus | Deliverable | Pass criteria |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Buyer discovery | 15 interviews with vertical AI labs/brands | 3 concrete data needs |
| 2 | Creator discovery | 30 creator/expert interviews | 10 willing to license |
| 3 | Domain pick | one domain (voice / K-beauty / expert) | buyer + creator supply match |
| 4 | Legal | license, consent, request terms | counsel sign-off |
| 5 | First request | buyer request live, rate card | 20 submissions |
| 6 | QC | manual + AI QC, acceptance | 60%+ acceptance |
| 7 | First license | dataset delivered, creator paid | revenue, zero disputes |
| 8 | Second buyer | second request, second domain test | 2 paid requests |
| 9 | Payout | KRW payouts, tax withholding | 10 creators paid |
| 10 | Repeat | creator re-submission, buyer repeat | 30% repeat |
| 11 | Case study | one buyer + one creator story | published |
| 12 | Go/no-go | metrics, legal, pipeline | seed or extend |

**Kill criteria:** no buyer pays; acceptance <40%; creators do not return; rights incident; gross margin <20%.

## 4. Buyers, creators, partners

**Buyers:** vertical AI labs, AI product teams, brands building domain models. Start with 3 design partners who have a live data need.

**Creators:** Korean creators, domain experts, small studios, clinics, agencies. Start with 30–50 in one domain.

**Partners:** KYC vendor, PII/illegal-content detection, cloud storage, PG/bank for payouts, legal counsel.

## 6. Risks

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Buyers do not pay enough | focus on high-value vertical/expert data |
| Creators do not return | fast payouts, reputation, request pipeline |
| Rights/consent | license terms, consent records, audit |
| QC cost | AI first, human on edge cases |
| Buyer concentration | 3+ buyers per domain |
| Competition from vendors | speed, vertical depth, creator payouts |
| Privacy incident | PII detection, minimization, deletion |

## 7. Fundraising

**Ask:** $500k–1M pre-seed for 12–18 months.

**Use:** 2 engineers, 1 supply lead, 1 QC/ops, legal, creator incentives.

**Seed milestones:** $100k GMV, 10 buyers, 500 creators, 30%+ take, zero rights incidents.
